# Annotated source directory

Sources consulted for the specification baseline of 25 November 2026. Scientific citations are locators for independently authored mathematics. Third-party text, diagrams, figures and datasets are not licensed for redistribution merely by appearing here. An original article and a handbook locator have different evidential weight; pending range extractions are recorded in the correlation register.

**[FNC]** Driscoll and Braun, Fundamentals of Numerical Computation. Consult chapters on linear least squares, roots, IVPs, BVPs and diffusion. Reference only. https://fncbook.com/

**[DOE]** US DOE, Fundamentals Handbook, Thermodynamics, Heat Transfer, and Fluid Flow, vol. 3, DOE-HDBK-1012/3-92. Fluid flow and Bernoulli/system head sections. Original illustrations only. https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/DOE-HDBK-1012-92_VOL3.pdf

**[BL]** MIT, Summary of Boundary Layer over a Flat Plate. Hydrodynamic similarity and local versus integrated measures. Consult as a scientific reference. https://web.mit.edu/13.021/13021_2003/lecturenotes/lecture18A.pdf

**[BLASIUS]** Blasius, The Boundary Layers in Fluids with Little Friction, translation of the 1908 paper. Historical similarity problem. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20050028493/downloads/20050028493.pdf

**[HEAT]** J. H. Lienhard V and IV, A Heat Transfer Textbook, 6th ed., v6.00 (2024). Steady and transient conduction, forced convection and heat exchangers. Copyrighted, limited personal/nonprofit instructional use. https://ahtt.mit.edu/

**[POHL]** E. Pohlhausen (1921), Der Wärmeaustausch zwischen festen Körpern und Flüssigkeiten mit kleiner Reibung und kleiner Wärmeleitung. Thermal boundary-layer source, DOI 10.1002/zamm.19210010205. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/zamm.19210010205

**[COLE]** C. F. Colebrook (1939), Turbulent flow in pipes, with particular reference to the transition region between the smooth and rough pipe laws. Distinguish smooth-to-rough transition from laminar-to-turbulent transition. https://www.emerald.com/jince/article/11/4/133/417643

**[ROUGH]** Colebrook and White (1937), Experiments with fluid friction in roughened pipes. DOI 10.1098/rspa.1937.0150. Original experimental context; rights to reproduce data require review. https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1937.0150

**[PUMP]** US DOE, Improving Pumping System Performance, 2nd ed. Pump and system interaction, operating costs and vendor duty evidence. https://www1.eere.energy.gov/manufacturing/tech_assistance/pdfs/pump.pdf

**[MIT]** MIT OCW 10.302 Transport Processes, Fall 2004, syllabus and references. Transport teaching sequence. https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/10-302-transport-processes-fall-2004/pages/syllabus/

**[FOG]** H. S. Fogler, Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering, companion site. Consult Conversion and Reactor Sizing, Isothermal Reactor Design, Nonisothermal Reactor Design and RTD sections. https://websites.umich.edu/~elements/5e/toc/index.html

**[IUPAC]** IUPAC Gold Book, Arrhenius equation, DOI 10.1351/goldbook.A00446. Terms have individual licensing; use own explanation. https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00446
