## C · Mathematical basis

Teaching line y=a+bx+ε; use QR least squares on [1,x]. s²=SSE/(n−2); leverage h*=1/n+(x*−x̄)²/Sxx; 95% mean CI: ŷ±t(.975,n−2)s√h*; prediction interval uses √(1+h*). Extension quadratic fit via QR on centred/scaled x and independent hold-out residuals.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

OLS assumes full-rank design, independent mean-zero homoscedastic errors; t-based intervals additionally assume normal errors. Range cannot establish correct functional form. Singular fits return reason. Imported data need units, origin and split metadata.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.

## J · Model alternatives and fidelity

NIST Norris data are real ozone-instrument calibration measurements; fit raw intercept and slope and compare certified values as an independent software check.

Starter cost: bounded numerical solve or sampled integration. Escalate when the omitted mechanism changes the decision by more than the uncertainty in the comparison, and when the additional data can be identified. See the fidelity and correlation standards for F-tag and evidence scope.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Fit a calibration line, reveal held-out points, and decide whether curvature or scatter is the larger problem. A high R² can coexist with poor extrapolation.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Increase sample count while keeping noise amplitude fixed. Confidence in mean typically improves; future observations retain irreducible scatter. R² is insufficient for extrapolation.

**Challenge and assessment.** Choose smallest dataset and degree with acceptable held-out error; optional curved truth y=1+2x+x² demonstrates bias.
