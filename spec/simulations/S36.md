## C · Mathematical basis

At fixed illustrative boiling point Tb=373 K, q=mcp(Tb−Ti)+mλf during boiling. For continuous flow replace mass by ṁ and energy by power. cp=4000 J/(kg K), λ=2e6 J/kg, Ti=293 K.
