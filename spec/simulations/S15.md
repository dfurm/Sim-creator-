## C · Mathematical basis

Ch=ṁh cp,h; Cc=ṁc cp,c; Cr=Cmin/Cmax; N=UA/Cmin; εparallel=[1−exp(−N(1+Cr))]/(1+Cr); εcounter=[1−exp(−N(1−Cr))]/[1−Cr exp(−N(1−Cr))]. At Cr=1 counter ε=N/(1+N). Q̇=εCmin(Thi−Tci); Tho=Thi−Q̇/Ch, Tco=Tci+Q̇/Cc.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Steady adiabatic exchanger, constant U and capacity rates, single phase, no axial mixing. Require Thi>Tci in heating exercise. Handle nearly equal capacity rates with expm1 and analytic limit. Fouling uses 1/Udirty=1/Uclean+Rf″ on a common area basis.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Reverse the cold stream while keeping both inlet conditions fixed. Compare the cold outlet with the hot outlet and the local approach temperature.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Swap arrangement at the same UA. Counterflow can produce a cold outlet hotter than the hot outlet because they occur at opposite ends; inspect same-end approach.
