## C · Mathematical basis

Sphere R=(1/ri−1/ro)/(4πk); fin with insulated tip η=tanh(mL)/(mL), m²=hP/(kAc). Composite parallel k=φk₁+(1−φ)k₂; series k=1/(φ/k₁+(1−φ)/k₂).
