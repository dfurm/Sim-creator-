## C · Mathematical basis

y*=αx/[1+(α−1)x]; xeq(y)=y/[α−(α−1)y]. Rectifying y=Rx/(R+1)+xD/(R+1); saturated-liquid q-line x=zF. Stripping line through (zF,yrect(zF)) and (xB,xB). Start (xD,xD), go horizontally to equilibrium then vertically to active line using x≥zF as rectifying. Stop after horizontal step reaches x≤xB.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Binary constant α, constant molar overflow, saturated liquid feed, total condenser, equilibrium stages. Require xB<zF<xD; Rmin from rectifying line through pinch (zF,y*(zF)) and (xD,xD) where physically admissible; block pinch or decreasing-step failure. Cap 500 stages and distinguish numerical cap from infeasibility.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.
