## I · Assumptions and applicability

External smooth isothermal flat plate, attached, low-speed, negligible natural convection, axial conduction and viscous dissipation; constant properties at a film state. Conservatively restrict Pr .6–10 and ReL<5×10⁵ with known laminar premise; unknown disturbance/transition remains explicit. Mixed convection requires buoyancy information.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.
