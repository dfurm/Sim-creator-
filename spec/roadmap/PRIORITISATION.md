# Prioritisation baseline

Score each criterion 1 (least favourable), 3 (moderate), 5 (most favourable), with 2 and 4 intermediate. Difficulty is student conceptual difficulty; higher means more reason to intervene. Build, verification and computation ease are reverse-burden scores. Alternative gap describes incremental value beyond a strong existing free option, not evidence of demand. Product/academic judgements, not market measurements. Weighted score = Σ(weight × score)/5 out of 100. Ties sorted by stable ID. Every component is in the CSV. Reweight by multiplying component columns by a new 100-point vector. Expansion ratings use subject-level priors adjusted for known rig links/advanced methods; revisit as lecturers score individual blocks.
