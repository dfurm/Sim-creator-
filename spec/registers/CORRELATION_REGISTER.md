# Correlation and model evidence register

The admissibility dimension includes source, geometry, independent-variable convention, fluid class, original data range, uncertainty, and alternative law. A teaching slider range never serves as the original data range. Unknown fields suppress the “within documented applicability” claim for real-service design. The following dispositions apply to all referenced blocks. Unquoted coefficients and algebra are written in the corresponding scientific specifications; published figures, tables and datasets are not bundled.

| Record and provenance | Basis, coefficients and conditions | Range, uncertainty, extrapolation and substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Pipe Darcy friction [COLE, ROUGH, DOE], S08 | Colebrook implicit law, uniform fully developed turbulent Newtonian flow in circular rough pipe. ε/D and pipe Re; 2.51 and 3.7 with base-10 log, Darcy convention. Historical roughened pipe evidence. | Teaching gate 4×10³–10⁷ and ε/D≤.05; this is an implementation envelope. Original specimen range/error bound needs independent extraction before claiming generic fit accuracy. Use laminar 64/Re where appropriate; transition unresolved, measured pressure drop for actual pipe. |
| Flat-plate momentum [BLASIUS, BL], S06 | Blasius similarity and computed constants Cf,x≈.664/√Reₓ, Cfavg≈1.328/√ReL. These follow from the similarity solution and are not fitted equipment coefficients. Edge defined at u/U=.99. | Smooth zero-pressure-gradient attached laminar geometry, no universal transition Re or universal uncertainty. Compare collocation, pressure-gradient solution or measured field. |
| Isothermal plate heat transfer [POHL, HEAT], S14 | Local .332 and average .664 times Re^½Pr^⅓; compact approximation to similarity solution for laminar forced convection. Use conductivity of the fluid; evaluate real properties at stated film temperature. | Pr=.6–10 as conservative teaching subset, Re bounded by plate transition rule; full source fit-error envelope pending reviewer. Confirm primary fit envelope/error against original article before quantitative service use. Alternative measured packing curve. |
| Sphere heat/mass transfer [RM], S83 | 2+.6 Re^½ Sc^⅓ or Pr^⅓, isolated particle and external-fluid properties. Original evaporation experiments concern drops. | Do not generalise drop evaporation to dense beds or large evaporation rates. Original article range and uncertainty not established from bibliographic retrospective. Research-only for external-duty design, compare pure diffusion limit Sh=2. |
| Pump, valve, meter, mixing [PUMP, IEC, PERRY], S09/S42/S47/S75 | H=s²H₀−aQ², Q=CdA₂√(…), valve G(x) or Np in P=NpρN³D⁵. Coefficients in the pack are illustrative, not vendor coefficients. | Synthetic defaults have no calibration uncertainty or fluid applicability claim. Require instrument/impeller ID, actual geometry, speed/viscosity window, a fitted dataset and withheld run. Use manufacturer data or a verified plant-specific curve. |
| Binary volatility / kinetics [SEP, IUPAC, FOG, MONOD, MM], S19–23/S41/S70–73 | Constant α and synthetic saturation pressures; Margules parameter, Arrhenius kref,Ea, growth and enzyme saturation parameters are scenario-specific. | Default is a teaching construction. Identify mixture/reaction and fit ranges before selecting a physical model. Alternatives are measured VLE, activity/fugacity models and expanded kinetics with independent data. |
| Adsorption, filtration and membrane [LANG, FILTER, VMAX, PERRY], S24/S62/S63/S78/S82 | Langmuir b,qmax, medium resistance, specific cake resistance, empirical capacity and mass-transfer coefficients are material/feed dependent. | No inherited coefficient validity across feeds/pressure/membranes. Check data fit and prediction intervals, cross-flow versus dead-end and compressibility; compare alternate resistance models and measured endpoints. |
| Antoine/property fits [NIST, NISTTERMS], later variants | log10(P in declared source unit)=A−B/(T+C), source T convention fixed. Data records include CAS/source/temperature interval and permitted use. | Coefficients for different ranges and units cannot be spliced silently. Actual WebBook records are linked, no coefficient table shipped. Check overlap, endpoints and redistribution terms. |
| Empirical teaching regression [STAT, STRD, NORRIS], S27 | Training and independent validation, units, x range, synthetic source flag or data provenance. Includes residuals, confidence/prediction intervals. | Assumptions on noise and design matter; extrapolation remains flagged even for a high R². Norris reference values are −.262323073774029 for the intercept and 1.00211681802045 for the slope are calculation benchmarks, not rig data. |

Correlation manifest requires original citation, equation identifier, parameter values and units, coefficient provenance, fitted materials/geometry, documented joint domain, property evaluation convention, uncertainty if reported or explicit unknown, alternative and data-rights decision. A cited handbook is a locator; engineering sign-off must inspect the actual correlation and original conditions.


### Additional source qualification

IAEA’s *Sodium Coolant Handbook*, table 31, gives a secondary reference envelope for the Colebrook–White form of 4×10³ to 10⁸ in Re and relative roughness up to .05. Furmtech deliberately narrows the upper Re to 10⁷ for the teaching controls. The handbook envelope is not an error guarantee for student pipework. [IAEA table 31](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/publications/PDF/CRCP_SOD_003web.pdf) · [Colebrook original](https://doi.org/10.1680/ijoti.1939.13150)

The Ergun original is indexed by UCSF and explicitly investigates flow, fluid, voidage and granular-solid characteristics. The published retrospective for Ranz and Marshall identifies drop diameters of 0.06–0.11 cm and air temperatures extending to 220 °C across the experiments. These are descriptions of the experimental programme, not blanket operating limits for every equation or fluid. [Ergun original record](https://www.industrydocuments.ucsf.edu/tid/efk76a99/pdf) · [Ranz–Marshall retrospective](https://garfield.library.upenn.edu/classics1993/A1993LB48900001.pdf)
