## Defined extension exercises

**Internal convection.** Add a separate S14 tube preset with smooth circular fully developed laminar flow, constant properties and negligible axial conduction, viscous dissipation and buoyancy. For a constant wall temperature NuD≈3.66, for constant wall heat flux NuD≈4.36. Inlet temperature, thermal development length and boundary type are required; do not apply either to an entrance region or to a flat plate. At kf=.60 W/(m K) and D=.020 m these give h≈109.8 or 130.8 W/(m² K). Screen hydrodynamic and thermal entrance lengths, not Re alone. For natural convection S52 displays Ra and calls for an orientation/BC-specific law rather than inferring h from the forced-flow model. [HEAT]

**Variable conductivity.** For one slab with k(T)=k₀[1+a(T−Tref)] and fixed face temperatures, q″=[∫Tc^Th k(T)dT]/L. For k₀=1 W/(m K), a=.01 K⁻¹, Tref=300 K, Tfaces 300/320 K, L=.1 m, heat flux magnitude=220 W/m². Reject nonpositive conductivity anywhere on the interval. Linear T(x) is then generally wrong. Recover it by integrating k(T) and solving for T at each x. [HEAT]

**Stage efficiency.** S23 counts an equilibrium partial reboiler separately. If only overall tray efficiency Eo is supplied for an illustration, actual trays=ceil((Nideal−1)/Eo), plus the separate reboiler; no stage for total condenser. With six ideal contacts and Eo=.70 use eight trays plus the reboiler. Murphree stage efficiencies instead use local composition changes; a single efficiency and this shortcut are inadequate for rigorous tray rating. [SEP, MCC]

**Coefficient fitting.** Compare a straight line with a power correlation Nu=a Reᵇ fit within measured conditions. Log-space regression changes the implied noise model. Use a direct nonlinear fit for additive errors in Nu; inspect residual and upper/lower-domain holdouts. NIST Norris verifies linear regression; Gauss2 tests numerical least-squares convergence and has a different functional form. Neither dataset supplies convection coefficients. [STRD, STAT]

**Natural convection and commissioning uncertainty.** For a temperature-controlled vertical plate, calculate RaL with properties at film temperature. A future vertical-plate model may use the Churchill–Chu average Nu relation; shipping it requires the chosen original formulation, correct Rayleigh range, geometry, uncertainty and sign-off. If a supplied h comes from a mixed or forced flow, show it as an input with provenance. Keep passive/conductive heat transfer distinct from mechanical circulation. [HEAT]
