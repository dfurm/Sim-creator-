## C · Mathematical basis

V dC/dt=Q(Cin−C)−kVC; D=Q/V; C∞=D Cin/(D+k); C(t)=C∞+(C₀−C∞)exp[−(D+k)t]. Fixed volume, equal inlet/outlet. Conservative tracer k=0.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Set k=0 and double Q; concentration approaches the same final feed concentration twice as fast. Reintroduce reaction and explain why the new plateau changes.
