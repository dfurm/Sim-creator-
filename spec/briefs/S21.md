## C · Mathematical basis

k(T)=kref exp[−Ea/R(1/T−1/Tref)]; slope of ln(k/kref) vs 1/T=−Ea/R. R=8.314462618 J/(mol K). Reference parameterisation reduces arbitrary prefactor scales.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Compare equal 10 K steps near 300 and 340 K. Their ratios differ because inverse temperature spacing differs.

## M · Verification and validation

At T=Tr k=.01 s⁻¹; at 310 K k≈.01909 s⁻¹. Increasing Ea at T=Tr leaves k fixed.

Verify dimensions and either an independent analytical limit, conservation identity or alternative numerical solve. Reject non-finite inputs and out-of-domain combinations in I. Use unrounded references; tolerances and regression process are in the QA standard. One independently computed prototype fixture is included in verification/reference_results.json. Physical validation requires independent observations in the declared domain; synthetic reference cases establish no equipment accuracy.
