## I · Assumptions and applicability

Steady smooth isothermal flat plate, attached laminar incompressible zero-pressure-gradient flow, uniform upstream velocity, constant properties. x≤L; x=0 singular. Reₓ near 5×10⁵ is a teaching screen, transition depends on surface and disturbance. Do not extrapolate Blasius to separation or use turbulent profiles near the wall as shear estimates.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.
