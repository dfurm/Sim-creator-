## C · Mathematical basis

A=πD²/4; v=Q/A; ṁ=ρQ. Two stations share Q by steady incompressible continuity. Positive axial direction downstream. Density fixed at 1000 kg/m³.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Pinch a pipe while holding throughput fixed. Predict which cross-section has greater mean velocity and whether mass flow changes.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Halve the downstream diameter. A twofold speed prediction misses the square in the area; the correct factor is four.

**Challenge and assessment.** Choose a bore to meet a velocity ceiling at fixed delivery; report feasible available bores. Explain why shrinking a real nozzle on a fixed pump may change Q. Submit a result with units, one rejected interpretation and the condition that would change the answer. Extension: Add a compressible density pair at known states, then a momentum/energy solution if pressures are sought.
