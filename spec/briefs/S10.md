## C · Mathematical basis

A=πD²/4; v=Q/A; Hreq=Δz+(fL/D+ΣK)v²/(2g); Pshaft=ρgQHreq/η. Use prescribed Darcy f for first release; advanced mode delegates to the friction block. ρ=1000, g=9.80665.
