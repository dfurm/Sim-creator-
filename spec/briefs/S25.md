## C · Mathematical basis

Synthetic plant τdy/dt=−y+Kpu+d. Each sample e=r−y; D=−Kc Td (y−yprev)/Ts filtered with β=Tf/(Tf+Ts), Df=βDfprev+(1−β)D; uraw=Kc e+I+Df; u=clip(uraw,0,1). I_next=I+Ts[Kc e/Ti+(u−uraw)/Tt] when back-calculation enabled. Plant exact ZOH update y_next=Kpu+d+(y−Kpu−d)exp(−Ts/τ). τ=10 s, Kp=1, Tf=.5 s, Tt=2 s, Ts=.1 s. Normalised variables.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.
