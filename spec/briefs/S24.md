## C · Mathematical basis

t=μRm V/(AΔp)+μαc V²/(2A²Δp); c is dry cake mass per filtrate volume. Write t=bv+av², v=V/A; stable v=2t/[b+√(b²+4at)], b=μRm/Δp, a=μαc/(2Δp). Areq=Vtarget/v. Instantaneous flux=Δp/[μ(Rm+αcv)].

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Constant pressure, Newtonian filtrate, incompressible homogeneous cake, constant retention; neglect startup hold-up and wash time. For compressible cake model α(Δp) with multiple-pressure data; invalid for cross-flow/TFF.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.
