## C · Mathematical basis

dp/dz=−ρg with z upward; p_abs=p_surface+ρgh for depth h down from the free surface. g=9.80665 m/s²; local atmosphere for gauge display fixed at 101325 Pa.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Static, uniform-density liquid, uniform gravity, negligible capillarity. Layered fluids require piecewise integration; gas columns require a density model. Pressure is scalar; arrows on walls indicate normal traction only.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.

## M · Verification and validation

At 2 m gauge=19613.3 Pa, absolute=120938.3 Pa. At h=0 p=p_surface. Increasing surface pressure adds a uniform offset.

Verify dimensions and either an independent analytical limit, conservation identity or alternative numerical solve. Reject non-finite inputs and out-of-domain combinations in I. Use unrounded references; tolerances and regression process are in the QA standard. One independently computed prototype fixture is included in verification/reference_results.json.
