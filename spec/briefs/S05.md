## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Drag a radial probe through the flow and reconstruct the mean from annular areas. Centreline velocity and cross-sectional mean are commonly confused.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. At r/R=.5 ask whether the point equals the mean. It is 1.5 times the mean; the mean occurs at r/R=1/√2.

**Challenge and assessment.** Find Q for a wall shear limit; determine whether the answer is laminar. Transfer to a viscous liquid at fixed Q and explain which field changes. Submit a result with units, one rejected interpretation and the condition that would change the answer. Extension: Use entrance length and measured pressure gradient for comparison; turbulent outer models require a different shear closure.
