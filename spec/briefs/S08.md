## C · Mathematical basis

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Full, straight, developed Newtonian liquid, constant bore. Laminar <2300; transition 2300–4000 suppress point estimate; turbulent ≥4000 within curated envelope Re≤10⁷ and ε/D≤.05. These upper limits are product guards, not a measured accuracy envelope. At Q=0 output Δp=0 and f undefined.
