## B · Educational purpose

Compare the surface of a thick slab with its centre. A good exponential fit to one sensor does not by itself establish uniform temperature.

Centre, surface and average traces make the lumped assumption testable across a changing Biot number.

Outcome: Solve elapsed time to a target strictly between initial and ambient. Reach equilibrium asymptotically; do not return a finite time at exactly ambient. ## C · Mathematical basis

Infinite slab of half-thickness L, two identical convecting faces, Lc=V/As=L. Bi=hL/k, α=k/(ρcp), Fo=αt/L²; lumped T=T∞+(Ti−T∞)exp[−h t/(ρcp L)]. For distributed comparator solve ∂T/∂t=αTxx; Tx(0)=0, −kTx(L)=h(Ts−T∞), initial T=Ti. ρcp=2.0×10⁶ J/(m³ K).

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Constant properties, convection on both faces, symmetric uniform initial condition, fluid at fixed temperature. Bi<.1 customary lumped screen; approaching/exceeding requires spatial comparison. Finite-volume implicit balance with actual face convection and half-cell conduction in series; refine grid/time.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.

## K · Engineering judgement and design

Solve elapsed time to a target strictly between initial and ambient. Reach equilibrium asymptotically; do not return a finite time at exactly ambient.

Document the target, remaining degrees of freedom, active constraints and feasible alternatives. Compare any inverse result by forward substitution; preserve infeasibility and multiple solutions. Cost requires a dated independent basis; use the physical trade-off otherwise.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Compare the surface of a thick slab with its centre. A good exponential fit to one sensor does not by itself establish uniform temperature.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Increase h tenfold, predict Bi and which sensor responds first. A single colour for the lumped slab communicates the model assumption. The surface responds ahead of the centre when cooling.

**Challenge and assessment.** Solve elapsed time to a target strictly between initial and ambient. Reach equilibrium asymptotically; do not return a finite time at exactly ambient. Submit a result with units, one rejected interpretation and the condition that would change the answer. Extension: A reference plane-wall eigenseries obeys λtanλ=Bi. Compare centre and mean separately; include sensor lag and uncertain bath h in experimental mode.

## M · Verification and validation

## N · Software implementation

Exact lumped branch. Spatial comparator: N=40 equal cells across half slab, backward Euler tridiagonal system; compare N=80 and half Δt. Initialise all cells at Ti; compute surface temperature from face resistance, not last cell centre.
