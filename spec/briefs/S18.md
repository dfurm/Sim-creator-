A tank receives a step in feed flow. Predict whether the level changes instantly and whether a new steady level exists.

## C · Mathematical basis

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Perfectly incompressible liquid, constant cross section, gravity or commanded discharge, vented headspace; gravity requires free discharge at datum. Adaptive time integration with event location for gravity mode; do not clip and discard volume.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** A tank receives a step in feed flow. Predict whether the level changes instantly and whether a new steady level exists.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Set equal constant flows and perturb the initial height. Pumped balance retains the new height; a gravity drain usually returns to a selected steady height.

**Challenge and assessment.** Find capacity required over a pulsed feed sequence; or determine gravity equilibrium (Qin/CdAo)²/(2g) and check vessel limits. Compare level-only vs separate outlet data. Submit a result with units, one rejected interpretation and the condition that would change the answer. Extension: Use measured outlet characteristic, geometry and dead volume for a rig. No clipping overflow losses without recording them.

## M · Verification and validation
