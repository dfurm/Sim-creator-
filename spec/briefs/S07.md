## B · Educational purpose

Hold velocity constant and double diameter, then repeat holding Q constant. Predict the opposite changes in Re and identify the variable the equipment actually holds.

Switching the controlled variable reverses the apparent diameter effect on the same Reynolds number.

Outcome: Choose D for a target Re under each constraint. Transfer to a stirred tank and identify the new characteristic velocity/length before using any correlation. Industrial teaching context: Piping, pumping and metering.

## C · Mathematical basis

Re=ρvD/μ=vD/ν; constant-Q mode derives v=4Q/(πD²). Default prescribed-speed mode. For external flow replace D with distance from the leading edge and use free-stream U.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

## L · Learning activities

**Explore.** Hold velocity constant and double diameter, then repeat holding Q constant. Predict the opposite changes in Re and identify the variable the equipment actually holds.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. When diameter doubles, choose what the pump holds. Students who answer “Re doubles” before identifying the constraint have insufficient information.

**Challenge and assessment.** Choose D for a target Re under each constraint. Transfer to a stirred tank and identify the new characteristic velocity/length before using any correlation. Submit a result with units, one rejected interpretation and the condition that would change the answer. Extension: Use characteristic scales from each geometry and independent evidence of regime; the ratio itself needs no calibration.

## M · Verification and validation

At default Re=2000. Double D at fixed v gives 4000; double D at fixed Q gives half Re. Re=0 is stationary.

Verify dimensions and either an independent analytical limit, conservation identity or alternative numerical solve. Reject non-finite inputs and out-of-domain combinations in I. Use unrounded references; tolerances and regression process are in the QA standard. One independently computed prototype fixture is included in verification/reference_results.json. Physical validation requires independent observations in the declared domain; synthetic reference cases establish no equipment accuracy.
