## C · Mathematical basis

Q(t)=b+mt; dQ/dt=m; V(t)−V(0)=bt+mt²/2. Use the signed difference of the antiderivative at the two cursors. Initial inventory zero for the default example.

Independent quantities and engineering units appear below. Outputs are derived. Assume SI absolute state variables unless a quantity is explicitly a difference or a gauge reading. Algebraic variants use the listed boundary state and require no time initialisation.

## I · Assumptions and applicability

Linear ramp, continuous commanded flow; reject a>t. Negative Q is an outward flow, suppress a physical fill animation if the integral demands negative inventory. Compare sampled trapezoids and noisy forward differences.

Apply per-output within/approaching/outside/unknown rules. Record assumed facts separately from verified input evidence. Unmodelled physical states or invalid dimensions suppress the corresponding output.

**Explore.** At what instant is a vessel filling fastest? Move a tangent and two time cursors on the same trace. Students often read the height of a flow trace as its accumulated volume.

**Investigate.** Commit a prediction before changing a control. Change b and leave m fixed. The slope is unchanged because b translates the graph. Compare two ramps with the same integrated volume.

## M · Verification and validation
